# Source and Evidence Policy

Status: AUTHORITATIVE

Use current primary sources for changeable facts, licenses/terms, standards, APIs, and vendor behavior. Distinguish quoted/source facts, empirical results, calculations, inferences, assumptions, and unresolved uncertainty.

Record enough provenance to reproduce material conclusions: source, version/date, method, scope, environment where relevant, result, and limitations. Public availability is not permission to copy. Do not turn old chats, historical CI, a different platform, plausible AI prose, or a manual test plan into current evidence.

Minimize copyrighted quotation and preserve required attribution. Internal/private sources remain subject to the privacy stop-gate and must not be transferred merely to support a public claim.
